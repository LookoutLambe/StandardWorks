Scriptures & Study Resources · Word-by-word Hebrew & English glosses

Study the Old and New Testaments, the Book of Mormon, Doctrine and Covenants, Pearl of Great Price, and Joseph Smith Translation in Hebrew—with interlinear translation, transliteration, and English glosses for each word. The Book of Mormon is rendered in pre-exilic Classical Biblical Hebrew, the Mikraic register of Isaiah and Jeremiah.
