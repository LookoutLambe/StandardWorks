The Hebrew Book of Mormon on paper — for the shelf, the study table, and gifts. Every edition is the same translation you read on this site: the whole Book of Mormon in pre-exilic Classical Biblical Hebrew, rendered by one hand. The web edition is free and always carries the latest corrections; the printed editions are the same text in five bindings.

The interlinear edition is the one to start with if you are learning: the English gloss sits under every Hebrew word. The full Hebrew edition is the text alone. The dual-language edition sets the Hebrew and the English on facing pages. The footnote edition is the hardcover, and the triple combination adds the Doctrine and Covenants and the Pearl of Great Price in Hebrew.
