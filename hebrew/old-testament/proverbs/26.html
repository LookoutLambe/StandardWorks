  1. 1

    כַּשֶּׁלֶגlike the snowבַּקַּיִץin the summerוְכַמָּטָרand like the rainבַּקָּצִירin the harvestכֵּןthusלֹא־נָאוֶהnot is suitableלִכְסִילfor a foolכָּבוֹד׃honor

    As snow in summer, and as rain in harvest, so honour is not seemly for a fool.

  2. 2

    כַּצִּפּוֹרlike the birdלָנוּדby wanderingכַּדְּרוֹרlike the swallowלָעוּףfor flyingכֵּןthusקִלְלַתthe curseחִנָּםcauseless(לא)[לוֹ]to himתָבֹא׃shall not come

    As the bird by wandering, as the swallow by flying, so the curse causeless shall not come.

  3. 3

    שׁוֹטa scourgeלַסּוּסto the horseמֶתֶגa bridleלַחֲמוֹרfor a donkeyוְשֵׁבֶטand a rodלְגֵוfor the back ofכְּסִילִים׃fools

    A whip for the horse, a bridle for the ass, and a rod for the fool's back.

  4. 4

    אַל־תַּעַןanswer notכְּסִילa foolכְּאִוַּלְתּוֹaccording to his foolishnessפֶּן־תִּשְׁוֶה־לּוֹlest you be like himגַם־אָתָּה׃also you are

    Answer not a fool according to his folly, lest thou also be like unto him.

  5. 5

    עֲנֵהanswerכְסִילa foolכְּאִוַּלְתּוֹaccording to his foolishnessפֶּן־יִהְיֶהlest he shall beחָכָםwiseבְּעֵינָיו׃in his eyes

    Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he be wise in his own conceit.

  6. 6

    מְקַצֶּהcuts offרַגְלַיִםfeetחָמָסdamageשֹׁתֶהdrinksשֹׁלֵחַhe who sendsדְּבָרִיםwordsבְּיַד־כְּסִיל׃by the hand of a fool

    He that sendeth a message by the hand of a fool cutteth off the feet, and drinketh damage.

  7. 7

    דַּלְיוּare not equalשֹׁקַיִםlegsמִפִּסֵּחַfrom a lame personוּמָשָׁלand a proverbבְּפִיin the mouth ofכְסִילִים׃fools

    The legs of the lame are not equal: so is a parable in the mouth of fools.

  8. 8

    כִּצְרוֹרas he who bindsאֶבֶןstoneבְּמַרְגֵּמָהin a slingכֵּן־נוֹתֵןso is he who givesלִכְסִילfor a foolכָּבוֹד׃honor

    As he that bindeth a stone in a sling, so is he that giveth honour to a fool.

  9. 9

    חוֹחַa thornעָלָהgoes upבְיַד־שִׁכּוֹרinto the hand of a drunkardוּמָשָׁלand a proverbבְּפִיin the mouth ofכְסִילִים׃fools

    As a thorn goeth up into the hand of a drunkard, so is a parable in the mouth of fools.

  10. 10

    רַבthe great Godמְחוֹלֵל־כֹּלwho formed all thingsוְשֹׂכֵרand rewardsכְּסִילthe foolוְשֹׂכֵרand rewardsעֹבְרִים׃transgressors

    The great God that formed all things both rewardeth the fool, and rewardeth transgressors.

  11. 11

    כְּכֶלֶבlike a dogשָׁבreturnsעַל־קֵאוֹupon his vomitכְּסִילa foolשׁוֹנֶהwho repeatsבְאִוַּלְתּוֹ׃his foolishness

    As a dog returneth to his vomit, so a fool returneth to his folly.

  12. 12

    רָאִיתָdo you seeאִישׁa manחָכָםwiseבְּעֵינָיוin his eyesתִּקְוָהthere is more hopeלִכְסִילfor a foolמִמֶּנּוּ׃from him

    Seest thou a man wise in his own conceit? there is more hope of a fool than of him.

  13. 13

    אָמַרsaidעָצֵלthe sluggardשַׁחַלa lionבַּדָּרֶךְby the wayאֲרִיa lionבֵּיןamongהָרְחֹבוֹת׃the streets

    The slothful man saith, There is a lion in the way; a lion is in the streets.

  14. 14

    הַדֶּלֶתthe doorתִּסּוֹבit turnsעַל־צִירָהּupon its hingesוְעָצֵלand a sluggardעַל־מִטָּתוֹ׃upon his bed

    As the door turneth upon his hinges, so doth the slothful upon his bed.

  15. 15

    טָמַןhe laidעָצֵלthe sluggardיָדוֹhis handבַּצַּלָּחַתin the dishנִלְאָהit grieves himלַהֲשִׁיבָהּto bring it againאֶל־פִּיו׃to his mouth

    The slothful hideth his hand in his bosom; it grieveth him to bring it again to his mouth.

  16. 16

    חָכָםis wiserעָצֵלthe sluggardבְּעֵינָיוin his eyesמִשִּׁבְעָהmore than sevenמְשִׁיבֵיwho renderטָעַם׃a reason

    The sluggard is wiser in his own conceit than seven men that can render a reason.

  17. 17

    מַחֲזִיקone who takes holdבְּאׇזְנֵי־כָלֶבin the ears of a dogעֹבֵרpassing byמִתְעַבֵּרmeddlesעַל־רִיבupon contentionלֹּא־לוֹ׃not his

    He that passeth by, and meddleth with strife belonging not to him, is like one that taketh a dog by the ears.

  18. 18

    כְּמִתְלַהְלֵהַּlike a madmanהַיֹּרֶהthe one who throwsזִקִּיםfirebrandsחִצִּיםarrowsוָמָוֶת׃and death

    As a mad man who casteth firebrands, arrows, and death,

  19. 19

    כֵּן־אִישׁthus man ofרִמָּהwho deceivesאֶת־רֵעֵהוּhis neighborוְאָמַרand saidהֲלֹא־מְשַׂחֵקis it not jestingאָנִי׃I

    So is the man that deceiveth his neighbour, and saith, Am not I in sport?

  20. 20

    בְּאֶפֶסwhere there is noעֵצִיםwoodתִּכְבֶּה־אֵשׁit is extinguished fireוּבְאֵיןand where there is noנִרְגָּןa slandererיִשְׁתֹּקceasesמָדוֹן׃strife

    Where no wood is, there the fire goeth out: so where there is no talebearer, the strife ceaseth.

  21. 21

    פֶּחָםcoalsלְגֶחָלִיםto burning coalsוְעֵצִיםand woodלְאֵשׁfor a fireוְאִישׁand a man(מדונים)[מִדְיָנִים]contentionsלְחַרְחַר־רִיב׃to kindling contention

    As coals are to burning coals, and wood to fire; so is a contentious man to kindle strife.

  22. 22

    דִּבְרֵיthe words ofנִרְגָּןa slandererכְּמִתְלַהֲמִיםare as woundsוְהֵםand theyיָרְדוּgo down intoחַדְרֵי־בָטֶן׃the chambers of the womb

    The words of a talebearer are as wounds, and they go down into the innermost parts of the belly.

  23. 23

    כֶּסֶףsilverסִיגִיםdrossמְצֻפֶּהoverlaidעַל־חָרֶשׂupon earthenwareשְׂפָתַיִםlipsדֹּלְקִיםburningוְלֶב־רָע׃and a wicked heart

    Burning lips and a wicked heart are like a potsherd covered with silver dross.

  24. 24

    בִּשְׂפָתָוwith his lipsיִנָּכֵרhe disguises himselfשׂוֹנֵאone who hatesוּבְקִרְבּוֹand in his midstיָשִׁיתhe will putמִרְמָה׃deceit

    He that hateth dissembleth with his lips, and layeth up deceit within him;

  25. 25

    כִּי־יְחַנֵּןwhen he speaks fairקוֹלוֹhis voiceאַל־תַּאֲמֶן־בּוֹbelieve him notכִּיforשֶׁבַעsevenתּוֹעֵבוֹתabominationsבְּלִבּוֹ׃in his heart

    When he speaketh fair, believe him not: for there are seven abominations in his heart.

  26. 26

    תִּכַּסֶּהit covers itselfשִׂנְאָהmaliceבְּמַשָּׁאוֹןby deceitתִּגָּלֶהit will be revealedרָעָתוֹhis evilבְקָהָל׃in the assembly

    Whose hatred is covered by deceit, his wickedness shall be shewed before the whole congregation.

  27. 27

    כֹּרֶה־שַּׁחַתis digging up a pitבָּהּin itיִפּוֹלshall fallוְגוֹלֵלand he who rollsאֶבֶןstoneאֵלָיוto himתָּשׁוּב׃return

    Whoso diggeth a pit shall fall therein: and he that rolleth a stone, it will return upon him.

  28. 28

    לְשׁוֹן־שֶׁקֶרthe language of falseיִשְׂנָאhe will hateדַכָּיוthose afflicted by itוּפֶהand a mouthחָלָקsmoothיַעֲשֶׂהhe shall doמִדְחֶה׃ruin

    A lying tongue hateth those that are afflicted by it; and a flattering mouth worketh ruin.
