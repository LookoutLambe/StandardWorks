    בֵּןa sonחָכָםwiseמוּסַרthe instruction ofאָבa fatherוְלֵץbut a scofferלֹא־שָׁמַעdoes not listen toגְּעָרָה׃a rebuke

    A wise son heareth his father's instruction: but a scorner heareth not rebuke.

  2. 2

    מִפְּרִיof fruit ofפִי־אִישׁthe mouth of a manיֹאכַלhe shall eatטוֹבgoodוְנֶפֶשׁbut the soul ofבֹּגְדִיםthe treacherousחָמָס׃violence

    A man shall eat good by the fruit of his mouth: but the soul of the transgressors shall eat violence.

  3. 3

    נֹצֵרhe who guardsפִּיוhis mouthשֹׁמֵרkeepsנַפְשׁוֹhis soulפֹּשֵׂקhe who opens wideשְׂפָתָיוhis lipsמְחִתָּה־לוֹ׃ruin is his

    He that keepeth his mouth keepeth his life: but he that openeth wide his lips shall have destruction.

  4. 4

    מִתְאַוָּהcravesוָאַיִןand has nothingנַפְשׁוֹhis soulעָצֵלthe sluggardוְנֶפֶשׁbut the soul ofחָרֻצִיםthe diligentתְּדֻשָּׁן׃it will be made fat

    The soul of the sluggard desireth, and hath nothing: but the soul of the diligent shall be made fat.

  5. 5

    דְּבַר־שֶׁקֶרa lying wordיִשְׂנָאhatesצַדִּיקthe righteousוְרָשָׁעand the wickedיַבְאִישׁacts loathsomelyוְיַחְפִּיר׃and he may act shamefully

    A righteous man hateth lying: but a wicked man is loathsome, and cometh to shame.

  6. 6

    צְדָקָהrighteousnessתִּצֹּרkeepsתׇּם־דָּרֶךְthe blameless in the wayוְרִשְׁעָהand wickednessתְּסַלֵּףoverthrowsחַטָּאת׃the sinner

    Righteousness keepeth him that is upright in the way: but wickedness overthroweth the sinner.

  7. 7

    יֵשׁthere isמִתְעַשֵּׁרone who makes himself richוְאֵיןyet has nothingכֹּלat allמִתְרוֹשֵׁשׁone who makes himself poorוְהוֹןyet has wealthרָב׃great

    There is that maketh himself rich, yet hath nothing: there is that maketh himself poor, yet hath great riches.

  8. 8

    כֹּפֶרa ransomנֶפֶשׁ־אִישׁof a man's lifeעׇשְׁרוֹis his richesוְרָשׁbut the poorלֹא־שָׁמַעhears noגְּעָרָה׃a rebuke

    The ransom of a man's life are his riches: but the poor heareth not rebuke.

  9. 9

    אוֹר־צַדִּיקִיםthe light of the righteousיִשְׂמָחrejoicesוְנֵרand the lamp ofרְשָׁעִיםthe wickedיִדְעָךְ׃it is extinguished

    The light of the righteous rejoiceth: but the lamp of the wicked shall be put out.

  10. 10

    רַק־בְּזָדוֹןonly by prideיִתֵּןcomesמַצָּהcontentionוְאֶת־נוֹעָצִיםbut with those who take adviceחׇכְמָה׃wisdom

    Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well advised is wisdom.

  11. 11

    Wealth gotten by vanity shall be diminished: but he that gathereth by labour shall increase.

  12. 12

    Hope deferred maketh the heart sick: but when the desire cometh, it is a tree of life.

  13. 13

    Whoso despiseth the word shall be destroyed: but he that feareth the commandment shall be rewarded.

  14. 14

    The law of the wise is a fountain of life, to depart from the snares of death.

  15. 15

    Good understanding giveth favour: but the way of transgressors is hard.

  16. 16

    Every prudent man dealeth with knowledge: but a fool layeth open his folly.

    A wicked messenger falleth into mischief: but a faithful ambassador is health.

  19. 19

    The desire accomplished is sweet to the soul: but it is abomination to fools to depart from evil.

    Evil pursueth sinners: but to the righteous good shall be repayed.
