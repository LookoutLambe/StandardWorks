  1. 1

    פַּלְגֵי־מַיִםstreams of waterלֶב־מֶלֶךְthe heart of a kingבְּיַד־יְהֹוָהis in the hand of the LORDעַל־כׇּל־אֲשֶׁרwhereverיַחְפֹּץHe willsיַטֶּנּוּ׃He turns it

    The king's heart is in the hand of the LORD, as the rivers of water: he turneth it whithersoever he will.

  2. 2

    כׇּל־דֶּרֶךְ־אִישׁevery way of a manיָשָׁרis rightבְּעֵינָיוin his own eyesוְתֹכֵןbut weighsלִבּוֹתthe heartsיְהֹוָה׃the LORD

    Every way of a man is right in his own eyes: but the LORD pondereth the hearts.

  3. 3

    עֲשֹׂהto doצְדָקָהrighteousnessוּמִשְׁפָּטand justiceנִבְחָרis more acceptableלַיהֹוָהto the LORDמִזָּבַח׃than sacrifice

    To do justice and judgment is more acceptable to the LORD than sacrifice.

  4. 4

    רוּם־עֵינַיִםhaughty eyesוּרְחַב־לֵבand a proud heartנִרthe plowing ofרְשָׁעִיםthe wickedחַטָּאת׃is sin

    An high look, and a proud heart, and the plowing of the wicked, is sin.

  5. 5

    מַחְשְׁבוֹתthe plans ofחָרוּץthe diligentאַךְ־לְמוֹתָרsurely to abundanceוְכׇל־אָץbut everyone who is hastyאַךְ־לְמַחְסוֹר׃surely to want

    The thoughts of the diligent tend only to plenteousness; but of every one that is hasty only to want.

  6. 6

    פֹּעַלthe getting ofאֹצָרוֹתtreasuresבִּלְשׁוֹןby a tongue ofשָׁקֶרfalsehoodהֶבֶלis a vaporנִדָּףdriven awayמְבַקְשֵׁי־מָוֶת׃of those who seek death

    The getting of treasures by a lying tongue is a vanity tossed to and fro of them that seek death.

  7. 7

    שֹׁד־רְשָׁעִיםthe robbery of the wickedיְגוֹרֵםwill drag them awayכִּיforמֵאֲנוּthey refuseלַעֲשׂוֹתto doמִשְׁפָּט׃justice

    The robbery of the wicked shall destroy them; because they refuse to do judgment.

  8. 8

    הֲפַכְפַּךְcrookedדֶּרֶךְis the way ofאִישׁa manוָזָרand strangeוְזַךְbut the pureיָשָׁרis rightפׇּעֳלוֹ׃his work

    The way of man is froward and strange: but as for the pure, his work is right.

  9. 9

    טוֹבit is betterלָשֶׁבֶתto dwellעַל־פִּנַּת־גָּגon a corner of a roofמֵאֵשֶׁתthan with a wife ofמִדְיָנִיםquarrelsוּבֵיתin a houseחָבֶר׃shared

    It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman in a wide house.

  10. 10

    נֶפֶשׁthe soul ofרָשָׁעthe wickedאִוְּתָה־רָעdesires evilלֹא־יֻחַןfinds no favorבְּעֵינָיוin his eyesרֵעֵהוּ׃his neighbor

    The soul of the wicked desireth evil: his neighbour findeth no favour in his eyes.

  11. 11

    בַּעֲנׇשׁ־לֵץwhen the scoffer is punishedיֶחְכַּם־פֶּתִיthe simple becomes wiseוּבְהַשְׂכִּילand when is instructedלְחָכָםthe wiseיִקַּח־דָּעַת׃he receives knowledge

    When the scorner is punished, the simple is made wise: and when the wise is instructed, he receiveth knowledge.

  12. 12

    מַשְׂכִּילconsidersצַדִּיקthe righteousלְבֵיתto the house ofרָשָׁעthe wickedמְסַלֵּףoverthrowingרְשָׁעִיםthe wickedלָרָע׃to ruin

    The righteous man wisely considereth the house of the wicked: but God overthroweth the wicked for their wickedness.

  13. 13

    אֹטֵםhe who shutsאׇזְנוֹhis earמִזַּעֲקַת־דָּלfrom the cry of the poorגַּם־הוּאhe tooיִקְרָאwill callוְלֹאand notיֵעָנֶה׃be answered

    Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, he also shall cry himself, but shall not be heard.

  14. 14

    מַתָּןa giftבַּסֵּתֶרin secretיִכְפֶּה־אָףaverts angerוְשֹׁחַדand a bribeבַּחֵקin the bosomחֵמָהwrathעַזָּה׃strong

    A gift in secret pacifieth anger: and a reward in the bosom strong wrath.

  15. 15

    שִׂמְחָהit is joyלַצַּדִּיקto the righteousעֲשׂוֹתto doמִשְׁפָּטjusticeוּמְחִתָּהbut terrorלְפֹעֲלֵיto the workers ofאָוֶן׃iniquity

    It is joy to the just to do judgment: but destruction shall be to the workers of iniquity.

  16. 16

    אָדָםa manתּוֹעֶהwho straysמִדֶּרֶךְfrom the way ofהַשְׂכֵּלunderstandingבִּקְהַלin the assembly ofרְפָאִיםthe shadesיָנוּחַ׃will rest

    The man that wandereth out of the way of understanding shall remain in the congregation of the dead.

  17. 17

    אִישׁa man ofמַחְסוֹרwantאֹהֵבis he who lovesשִׂמְחָהpleasureאֹהֵבhe who lovesיַיִן־וָשֶׁמֶןwine and oilלֹאnotיַעֲשִׁיר׃will be rich

    He that loveth pleasure shall be a poor man: he that loveth wine and oil shall not be rich.

  18. 18

    כֹּפֶרis a ransomלַצַּדִּיקfor the righteousרָשָׁעthe wickedוְתַחַתand in place ofיְשָׁרִיםthe uprightבּוֹגֵד׃the treacherous

    The wicked shall be a ransom for the righteous, and the transgressor for the upright.

  19. 19

    טוֹבit is betterשֶׁבֶתto dwellבְּאֶרֶץ־מִדְבָּרin a desert landמֵאֵשֶׁתthan with a wife of(מדונים)[מִדְיָנִים]quarrelsוָכָעַס׃and vexation

    It is better to dwell in the wilderness, than with a contentious and an angry woman.

  20. 20

    אוֹצָרtreasureנֶחְמָדdesirableוָשֶׁמֶןand oilבִּנְוֵהis in the dwelling ofחָכָםthe wiseוּכְסִילbut a fool ofאָדָםa manיְבַלְּעֶנּוּ׃swallows it up

    There is treasure to be desired and oil in the dwelling of the wise; but a foolish man spendeth it up.

  21. 21

    רֹדֵףhe who pursuesצְדָקָהrighteousnessוָחָסֶדand kindnessיִמְצָאwill findחַיִּיםlifeצְדָקָהrighteousnessוְכָבוֹד׃and honor

    He that followeth after righteousness and mercy findeth life, righteousness, and honour.

  22. 22

    עִירthe city ofגִּבֹּרִיםthe mightyעָלָהscalesחָכָםa wise manוַיֹּרֶדand brings downעֹזthe strength ofמִבְטֶחָהֿ׃its confidence

    A wise man scaleth the city of the mighty, and casteth down the strength of the confidence thereof.

  23. 23

    שֹׁמֵרhe who keepsפִּיוhis mouthוּלְשׁוֹנוֹand his tongueשֹׁמֵרkeepsמִצָּרוֹתfrom troublesנַפְשׁוֹ׃his soul

    Whoso keepeth his mouth and his tongue keepeth his soul from troubles.

  24. 24

    זֵדthe proudיָהִירarrogantלֵץscoffer isשְׁמוֹhis nameעוֹשֶׂהwho actsבְּעֶבְרַתin the fury ofזָדוֹן׃pride

    Proud and haughty scorner is his name, who dealeth in proud wrath.

  25. 25

    The desire of the slothful killeth him; for his hands refuse to labour.

  26. 26

    כׇּל־הַיוֹםall the dayהִתְאַוָּהhe cravesתַאֲוָהcravingוְצַדִּיקbut the righteousיִתֵּןgivesוְלֹאand does notיַחְשֹׂךְ׃hold back

    He coveteth greedily all the day long: but the righteous giveth and spareth not.

  27. 27

    זֶבַחthe sacrifice ofרְשָׁעִיםthe wickedתּוֹעֵבָהis an abominationאַףhow much moreכִּי־בְזִמָּהwhen with evil intentיְבִיאֶנּוּ׃he brings it

    The sacrifice of the wicked is abomination: how much more, when he bringeth it with a wicked mind?

  28. 28

    A false witness shall perish: but the man that heareth speaketh constantly.

  29. 29

    A wicked man hardeneth his face: but as for the upright, he directeth his way.

  30. 30

    There is no wisdom nor understanding nor counsel against the LORD.

  31. 31

    The horse is prepared against the day of battle: but safety is of the LORD.
