  1. 1

    טוֹב־רָשׁbetter is the poorהוֹלֵךְwho walksבְּתֻמּוֹin his integrityמֵעִקֵּשׁthan one perverse inשְׂפָתָיוhis lipsוְהוּאand he isכְסִיל׃a fool

    Better is the poor that walketh in his integrity, than he that is perverse in his lips, and is a fool.

  2. 2

    גַּםalsoבְּלֹא־דַעַתwithout knowledgeנֶפֶשׁfor the soulלֹא־טוֹבis not goodוְאָץand he who hastensבְּרַגְלַיִםwith his feetחוֹטֵא׃sins

    Also, that the soul be without knowledge, it is not good; and he that hasteth with his feet sinneth.

  3. 3

    אִוֶּלֶתthe folly ofאָדָםa manתְּסַלֵּףsubvertsדַּרְכּוֹhis wayוְעַל־יְהֹוָהand against the LORDיִזְעַףragesלִבּוֹ׃his heart

    The foolishness of man perverteth his way: and his heart fretteth against the LORD.

  4. 4

    הוֹןwealthיֹסִיףaddsרֵעִיםfriendsרַבִּיםmanyוְדָלbut the poorמֵרֵעֵהוּfrom his friendיִפָּרֵד׃is separated

    Wealth maketh many friends; but the poor is separated from his neighbour.

  5. 5

    עֵדa witness ofשְׁקָרִיםliesלֹאnotיִנָּקֶהwill go unpunishedוְיָפִיחַand he who breathes outכְּזָבִיםliesלֹאnotיִמָּלֵט׃will escape

    A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that speaketh lies shall not escape.

  6. 6

    רַבִּיםmanyיְחַלּוּentreatפְנֵי־נָדִיבthe favor of a princeוְכׇל־הָרֵעַand everyone is a friendלְאִישׁto a man ofמַתָּן׃gifts

    Many will intreat the favour of the prince: and every man is a friend to him that giveth gifts.

  7. 7

    כׇּל־אֲחֵי־רָשׁall the brothers of the poorשְׂנֵאֻהוּhate himאַףhow much moreכִּיdoמְרֵעֵהוּhis friendsרָחֲקוּgo farמִמֶּנּוּfrom himמְרַדֵּףhe pursuesאֲמָרִיםwith words(לא)[לוֹ־]הֵמָּה׃to him they are

    All the brethren of the poor do hate him: how much more do his friends go far from him? he pursueth them with words, yet they are wanting to him.

  8. 8

    קֹנֶה־לֵּבhe who gets senseאֹהֵבlovesנַפְשׁוֹhis own soulשֹׁמֵרhe who keepsתְּבוּנָהunderstandingלִמְצֹא־טוֹב׃will find good

    He that getteth wisdom loveth his own soul: he that keepeth understanding shall find good.

  9. 9

    עֵדa witness ofשְׁקָרִיםliesלֹאnotיִנָּקֶהwill go unpunishedוְיָפִיחַand he who breathes outכְּזָבִיםliesיֹאבֵד׃will perish

    A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that speaketh lies shall perish.

  10. 10

    לֹא־נָאוֶהis not fittingלִכְסִילfor a foolתַּעֲנוּגluxuryאַףhow much lessכִּי־לְעֶבֶדfor a servantמְשֹׁלto ruleבְּשָׂרִים׃over princes

    Delight is not seemly for a fool; much less for a servant to have rule over princes.

  11. 11

    שֵׂכֶלthe discretion ofאָדָםa manהֶאֱרִיךְmakes slowאַפּוֹhis angerוְתִפְאַרְתּוֹand it is his gloryעֲבֹרto pass overעַל־פָּשַׁע׃a transgression

    The discretion of a man deferreth his anger; and it is his glory to pass over a transgression.

  12. 12

    נַהַםis a roaringכַּכְּפִירlike the young lionזַעַףis the rage ofמֶלֶךְa kingוּכְטַלbut like dewעַל־עֵשֶׂבon the grassרְצוֹנוֹ׃is his favor

    The king's wrath is as the roaring of a lion; but his favour is as dew upon the grass.

  13. 13

    הַוֺּתis ruinלְאָבִיוto his fatherבֵּןa sonכְּסִילfoolishוְדֶלֶףand a drippingטֹרֵדcontinualמִדְיְנֵיare the quarrels ofאִשָּׁה׃a wife

    A foolish son is the calamity of his father: and the contentions of a wife are a continual dropping.

  14. 14

    בַּיִתhouseוָהוֹןand wealthנַחֲלַתare the inheritance ofאָבוֹתfathersוּמֵיְהֹוָהbut from the LORDאִשָּׁהis a wifeמַשְׂכָּלֶת׃prudent

    House and riches are the inheritance of fathers: and a prudent wife is from the LORD.

  15. 15

    עַצְלָהlazinessתַּפִּילcasts intoתַּרְדֵּמָהa deep sleepוְנֶפֶשׁand a soulרְמִיָּהslackתִרְעָב׃will hunger

    Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep; and an idle soul shall suffer hunger.

  16. 16

    שֹׁמֵרhe who keepsמִצְוָהthe commandmentשֹׁמֵרkeepsנַפְשׁוֹhis soulבּוֹזֵהhe who despisesדְרָכָיוhis ways(יומת)[יָמוּת]׃will die

    He that keepeth the commandment keepeth his own soul; but he that despiseth his ways shall die.

  17. 17

    מַלְוֵהlends toיְהֹוָהthe LORDחוֹנֵןhe who is gracious toדָּלthe poorוּגְמֻלוֹand his good deedיְשַׁלֶּם־לוֹ׃He will repay him

    He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the LORD; and that which he hath given will he pay him again.

  18. 18

    יַסֵּרdisciplineבִּנְךָyour sonכִּי־יֵשׁwhile there isתִּקְוָהhopeוְאֶל־הֲמִיתוֹbut on putting him to deathאַל־תִּשָּׂאdo not setנַפְשֶׁךָ׃your soul

    Chasten thy son while there is hope, and let not thy soul spare for his crying.

  19. 19

    (גרל)[גְּדׇל־]חֵמָהgreat of wrathנֹשֵׂאwill bearעֹנֶשׁthe penaltyכִּיforאִם־תַּצִּילif you rescue himוְעוֹדand againתּוֹסִף׃you will add

    A man of great wrath shall suffer punishment: for if thou deliver him, yet thou must do it again.

  20. 20

    שְׁמַעlisten toעֵצָהcounselוְקַבֵּלand receiveמוּסָרinstructionלְמַעַןso thatתֶּחְכַּםyou may be wiseבְּאַחֲרִיתֶךָ׃in your latter end

    Hear counsel, and receive instruction, that thou mayest be wise in thy latter end.

  21. 21

    רַבּוֹתmany areמַחֲשָׁבוֹתthe plansבְּלֶב־אִישׁin the heart of a manוַעֲצַתbut the counsel ofיְהֹוָהthe LORDהִיאitתָקוּם׃will stand

    There are many devices in a man's heart; nevertheless the counsel of the LORD, that shall stand.

  22. 22

    תַּאֲוַתwhat is desired inאָדָםa manחַסְדּוֹis his kindnessוְטוֹב־רָשׁand better is a poor manמֵאִישׁthan a man ofכָּזָב׃a lie

    The desire of a man is his kindness: and a poor man is better than a liar.

  23. 23

    יִרְאַתthe fear ofיְהֹוָהthe LORDלְחַיִּיםis to lifeוְשָׂבֵעַand satisfiedיָלִיןhe will restבַּל־יִפָּקֶדhe will not be visitedרָע׃by evil

    The fear of the LORD tendeth to life: and he that hath it shall abide satisfied; he shall not be visited with evil.

  24. 24

    טָמַןburiesעָצֵלthe sluggardיָדוֹhis handבַּצַּלָּחַתin the dishגַּם־אֶל־פִּיהוּeven to his mouthלֹאnotיְשִׁיבֶנָּה׃will bring it back

    A slothful man hideth his hand in his bosom, and will not so much as bring it to his mouth again.

  25. 25

    לֵץa scofferתַּכֶּהstrikeוּפֶתִיand the simpleיַעְרִםwill become prudentוְהוֹכִיחַand reproveלְנָבוֹןone who is discerningיָבִיןand he will understandדָּעַת׃knowledge

    Smite a scorner, and the simple will beware: and reprove one that hath understanding, and he will understand knowledge.

  26. 26

    He that wasteth his father, and chaseth away his mother, is a son that causeth shame, and bringeth reproach.

  27. 27

    חֲדַל־בְּנִיcease my sonלִשְׁמֹעַto hearמוּסָרinstructionלִשְׁגוֹתonly to strayמֵאִמְרֵי־דָעַת׃from the words of knowledge

    Cease, my son, to hear the instruction that causeth to err from the words of knowledge.

  28. 28

    עֵדa witness ofבְּלִיַּעַלworthlessnessיָלִיץmocksמִשְׁפָּטjusticeוּפִיand the mouth ofרְשָׁעִיםthe wickedיְבַלַּע־אָוֶן׃swallows iniquity

    An ungodly witness scorneth judgment: and the mouth of the wicked devoureth iniquity.

  29. 29

    נָכוֹנוּare preparedלַלֵּצִיםfor scoffersשְׁפָטִיםjudgmentsוּמַהֲלֻמוֹתand blowsלְגֵוfor the back ofכְּסִילִים׃fools

    Judgments are prepared for scorners, and stripes for the back of fools.
