  1. 1

    הַלְלוּ־יָהּpraise the LORDהַלְלוּ־אֵלpraise Godבְּקׇדְשׁוֹin His sanctuaryהַלְלוּהוּpraise Himבִּרְקִיעַin the firmament ofעֻזּוֹ׃His power

    Praise ye the LORD. Praise God in his sanctuary: praise him in the firmament of his power.

  2. 2

    הַלְלוּהוּpraise Himבִגְבוּרֹתָיוfor His mighty actsהַלְלוּהוּpraise Himכְּרֹבaccording to the multitude ofגֻּדְלוֹ׃His greatness

    Praise him for his mighty acts: praise him according to his excellent greatness.

  3. 3

    הַלְלוּהוּpraise Himבְּתֵקַעwith the blast ofשׁוֹפָרthe hornהַלְלוּהוּpraise Himבְּנֵבֶלwith lyreוְכִנּוֹר׃and harp

    Praise him with the sound of the trumpet: praise him with the psaltery and harp.

  4. 4

    הַלְלוּהוּpraise Himבְּתֹףwith tambourineוּמָחוֹלand danceהַלְלוּהוּpraise Himבְּמִנִּיםwith stringed instrumentsוְעֻגָב׃and pipe

    Praise him with the timbrel and dance: praise him with stringed instruments and organs.

  5. 5

    הַלְלוּהוּpraise Himבְצִלְצְלֵי־שָׁמַעupon the loud cymbalsהַלְלוּהוּpraise Himבְּצִלְצְלֵיupon the cymbals ofתְרוּעָה׃loud clanging

    Praise him upon the loud cymbals: praise him upon the high sounding cymbals.

  6. 6

    כֹּלlet everyהַנְּשָׁמָהthing that has breathתְּהַלֵּלpraiseיָהּthe LORDהַלְלוּ־יָהּ׃praise the LORD

    Let every thing that hath breath praise the LORD. Praise ye the LORD.
