    A Psalm of Asaph. God standeth in the congregation of the mighty; he judgeth among the gods.

  2. 2

    עַד־מָתַיhow longתִּשְׁפְּטוּ־עָוֶלwill you judge unjustlyוּפְנֵיand the persons ofרְשָׁעִיםthe wickedתִּשְׂאוּ־סֶלָה׃will you favor Selah

    How long will ye judge unjustly, and accept the persons of the wicked? Selah.

  3. 3

    שִׁפְטוּ־דַלdefend the poorוְיָתוֹםand the fatherlessעָנִיthe afflictedוָרָשׁand needyהַצְדִּיקוּ׃do justice to

    Defend the poor and fatherless: do justice to the afflicted and needy.

  4. 4

    פַּלְּטוּ־דַלdeliver the poorוְאֶבְיוֹןand needyמִיַּדfrom the hand ofרְשָׁעִיםthe wickedהַצִּילוּ׃deliver them

    Deliver the poor and needy: rid them out of the hand of the wicked.

  5. 5

    They know not, neither will they understand; they walk on in darkness: all the foundations of the earth are out of course.

  6. 6

    אֲנִי־אָמַרְתִּיI saidאֱלֹהִיםgodsאַתֶּםyou areוּבְנֵיand sons ofעֶלְיוֹןthe Most Highכֻּלְּכֶם׃all of you

    I have said, Ye are gods; and all of you are children of the most High.

  7. 7

    אָכֵןsurelyכְּאָדָםlike menתְּמוּתוּןyou shall dieוּכְאַחַדand like one ofהַשָּׂרִיםthe princesתִּפֹּלוּ׃you shall fall

    But ye shall die like men, and fall like one of the princes.

  8. 8

    קוּמָהariseאֱלֹהִיםO Godשׇׁפְטָהjudgeהָאָרֶץthe earthכִּי־אַתָּהfor Youתִנְחַלshall inheritבְּכׇל־הַגּוֹיִם׃all the nations

    Arise, O God, judge the earth: for thou shalt inherit all nations.
