    A Psalm of David. LORD, I cry unto thee: make haste unto me; give ear unto my voice, when I cry unto thee.

    Let my prayer be set forth before thee as incense; and the lifting up of my hands as the evening sacrifice.

    Set a watch, O LORD, before my mouth; keep the door of my lips.

  4. 4

    אַל־תַּט־לִבִּיincline not my heartלְדָבָרto any thingרָעevilלְהִתְעוֹלֵלto practiseעֲלִלוֹתdeedsבְּרֶשַׁעin wickednessאֶת־אִישִׁיםwith menפֹּעֲלֵי־אָוֶןwho work iniquityוּבַל־אֶלְחַםand let me not eatבְּמַנְעַמֵּיהֶם׃of their dainties

    Incline not my heart to any evil thing, to practise wicked works with men that work iniquity: and let me not eat of their dainties.

  5. 5

    Let the righteous smite me; it shall be a kindness: and let him reprove me; it shall be an excellent oil, which shall not break my head: for yet my prayer also shall be in their calamities.

  6. 6

    נִשְׁמְטוּare thrown downבִידֵי־סֶלַעby the sides of the rockשֹׁפְטֵיהֶםtheir judgesוְשָׁמְעוּand they shall hearאֲמָרַיmy wordsכִּיforנָעֵמוּ׃they are sweet

    When their judges are overthrown in stony places, they shall hear my words; for they are sweet.

  7. 7

    כְּמוֹas whenפֹלֵחַone plowsוּבֹקֵעַand cleavesבָּאָרֶץon the earthנִפְזְרוּare scatteredעֲצָמֵינוּour bonesלְפִיat the mouth ofשְׁאוֹל׃Sheol

    Our bones are scattered at the grave's mouth, as when one cutteth and cleaveth wood upon the earth.

  8. 8

    כִּיforאֵלֶיךָto YouיֱהֹוִהO GODאֲדֹנָיthe Lordעֵינָיare my eyesבְּכָהin YouחָסִיתִיI put my trustאַל־תְּעַרleave not destituteנַפְשִׁי׃my soul

    But mine eyes are unto thee, O GOD the Lord: in thee is my trust; leave not my soul destitute.

  9. 9

    Keep me from the snares which they have laid for me, and the gins of the workers of iniquity.

  10. 10

    יִפְּלוּlet fallבְמַכְמֹרָיוinto their own netsרְשָׁעִיםthe wickedיַחַדtogetherאָנֹכִיwhile Iעַד־אֶעֱבוֹר׃escape

    Let the wicked fall into their own nets, whilst that I withal escape.
